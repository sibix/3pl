PDF Utility

Reorder PDF pages by barcode

Upload a PDF where each page carries a barcode, then give the order you want (a list of barcode numbers). This tool reads the barcode on every page and rebuilds the PDF in that order — entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1
Upload the PDF

Each page should contain a scannable barcode (1D formats: Code128, EAN, UPC, Code39, Codabar, ITF).
